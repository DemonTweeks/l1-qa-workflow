## Rejection Triggers (Global)

Conditions that cause an immediate REJECT (unless a section explicitly relaxes them):

1. Staging/fraud in Before images (clear and convincing evidence)
2. Missing mandatory checklist items with no explanation (verified N/A across all images due to absence, not just angle)
3. Critical water/ingress failure with no waterproofing on outdoor connectors
4. Safety hazard: missing grounding where explicitly required and clearly visible
5. Intentional falsification: same equipment shown in Before and After with no change

Note: Individual section rules may add or relax specific triggers; always check the section's To Don'ts first.
